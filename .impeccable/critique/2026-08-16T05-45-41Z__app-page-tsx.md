## Design Specificity Verdict

The hero is authored for this product. The rest is not yet.

Measured: the hero H1 renders 62.4px w900 UPPERCASE. All six remaining section headings render 49.6px w600 sentence-case. The hero uses 0 cool-token elements and 2 warm; Curriculum uses 32 cool against 16 warm; Path 6 cool. Two light laws coexist: buttons, the portrait frame and the story ring use vertical/conic light; every card, tag and panel still uses the original 148° diagonal.

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs over components/sections, app/page.tsx, app/layout.tsx, Pill, Countdown, Icons, Liquid → `[]`, exit 0. No mechanical findings. The unity problem is a design-system decision the detector cannot see.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The page reads as two products stitched together.** Hero: black ground, warm flame, uppercase w900, colourless glass. Everything below: cool blue/indigo/purple ambient, sentence-case w600, diagonal glass. Fix: carry the hero's law downward — one light direction (vertical), one heading voice, warm accents replacing teal/purple/pink. Curriculum and Path are the heaviest offenders (32 and 6 cool elements).

**[P1] Zero social proof on a page asking $100–390.** content.json has empty shots/testimonials/videos/graduates, so the Proof section does not render at all. Correct per the "no invented proof" principle, but it leaves the highest-stakes moment — choosing a package — with nothing but the author's own claim. Fix: get any real material in via admin; even 2 screenshots plus a graduate count changes the page.

**[P1] Two light laws.** `.glass::after` / `.tag::after` light at 148°; `.pill::after`, `.portrait-frame::after` light at 180°. Side by side the cards look lit from a different sun than the buttons. Fix: move cards and tags to the vertical law.

**[P2] Touch targets under the 44px minimum.** Measured at 375px: "Kursga yozilish" (nav) and "Paketlarni ko'rish" (Pain) render 40px tall — `.pill-sm { min-height: 40px }`. The audience is phone-first. Fix: raise `.pill-sm` to 44px.

**[P2] PRO price inversion.** $390 shown as the biggest discount against a $300 original. The author chose to keep it, but on a page whose entire job is trust it reads as an error.

## What's Working

- **The hero's material is genuinely specific.** The flame, grid and dithered grain were pixel-measured from the author's reference, not approximated. The portrait's own blurred copy lighting the frame ties subject and background into one system.
- **Language.** Plain Uzbek, no jargon, matched to a non-technical audience. Heuristic 2 is a real 4.
- **Contrast holds.** text-2 = 11.17:1, text-3 = 5.66:1 on black. Both pass AA.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** hits the packages with no testimonial, no graduate count, no income proof anywhere on the page. The only evidence is the author's own bio. Clicks a package button and is thrown into Telegram with no warning that the site has no checkout.

**Casey (distracted mobile):** two CTAs are 40px tall, under the 44px thumb minimum. The hero is 1155px on a 812px screen, so the portrait sits below the fold.

**Riley (stress tester):** finds PRO's "biggest discount" ($390) costs more than its original price ($300). Also notices the Proof section is simply absent rather than explained.
